# United States v. Philip Morris USA Inc. — Amended Final Opinion

Judge Gladys Kessler's amended final opinion in the United States' civil RICO
action against the major cigarette manufacturers, U.S. District Court for the
District of Columbia, Civil Action No. 99-CV-2496 (GK), filed 17 August 2006
(amended opinion filed 8 September 2006). 1,682 pages of findings of fact and
conclusions of law.

## Source

`archive/final-opinion.pdf` — as published by the U.S. Department of Justice at
<https://www.justice.gov/civil/case/us-v-philip-morris-usa-inc-et-al>. Public
domain (work of the U.S. federal courts / Government). See `datapackage.json`.
